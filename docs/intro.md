# Welcome

This is a living reference for internal medicine shelf exam preparation.

## How this notebook is organized

Content is organized by **organ system** → **condition**. Within each condition, you'll find consistent sections:

- **What to Know** — the high-yield summary
- **Most Commonly Tested** — shelf exam focus points
- **Physiology & Pathophysiology** — mechanisms you need to understand
- **Diagnosis** — workup, labs, imaging
- **Management** — acute and chronic treatment
- **Common Test Questions** — practice vignettes with explanations
- **Videos & Resources** — curated external links
- **Pearls & Pitfalls** — easy misses and buzzwords

## How to use it

- Browse by organ system in the left sidebar
- Use the **search bar** at the top to jump to any topic
- Check the "last updated" date at the bottom of each page
